About This App

ATX Quote Builder v1.0
Internal pricing tool for AeroTerraX Land Solutions field scoping conversations.

Implements the formula-based pricing framework (Base Fee + Per-Unit Rate × Count + Complexity Multiplier) documented in ATX-PRICE-001 v1.0. NRCS payment envelopes sourced from Missouri EQIP-1 cost list, 2025-2026. Pre-certification pricing structure; will require revision upon TSP certification activation.

Data storage: All quote history and pricing tuning is stored locally on this device using browser localStorage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing browser data will erase both.

Email delivery: The "Email Summary to Client" button opens your phone's default mail app with a pre-filled quote summary. You review and tap Send. The email comes from your phone's mail account and appears in your Sent folder.

Companion documents: ATX-PRICE-001 v1.0 (Word document, full framework); ATX-PRICE-001 Quote Builder (Excel workbook, office estimate development).

Deploy to GitHub Pages

  1. Create a new GitHub repo (public or private).
  2. Upload this file as index.html.
  3. In repo Settings → Pages, enable Pages from main branch.
  4. Access via https://[username].github.io/[reponame]/.
  5. On your phone, open in Safari or Chrome, then "Add to Home Screen" for one-tap access.
